# Topics, scope and priorities

Order in the email: Formula 1 → UFC → Tennis → Cricket.

## Formula 1
- Include: the latest Grand Prix (and Sprint, if any) in the last 7 days — podium, top-5 if
  interesting, 2–3 highlights (safety cars, crashes, strategy, championship swing).
- Include: championship standings impact (leader and gap).
- Include: top news only — driver contracts/seat changes, injuries, penalties, team boss changes.
- Upcoming: next Grand Prix with race start in IST (add qualifying time if it fits).
- Exclude: F2/F3, IndyCar, technical deep-dives, rumours.
- Good sources: formula1.com, fia.com, skysports.com/f1, the-race.com, autosport.com.

## UFC
- Include: results of numbered events and Fight Nights in the last 7 days — main event
  (winner, method, round), co-main, and 1–2 other notable results.
- Include: prominent news only — title changes, vacated belts, big fights announced,
  main-event cancellations/injuries.
- Upcoming: next event — main event, co-main, rest of main card, main-card start in IST
  (UFC lists ET: add 9h30m in EDT / 10h30m in EST; US Saturday nights are Sunday mornings in IST).
- Exclude: regional MMA, other promotions, gossip.
- Good sources: ufc.com, espn.com/mma, cbssports.com/ufc, mmamania.com, bloodyelbow.com.

## Tennis (Grand Slams only)
- Priority 1: Men's singles. Priority 2: Women's singles. Doubles/mixed/juniors: only if
  genuinely historic.
- Include: Slam results in the last 7 days (during a Slam: daily key results of top seeds,
  from QF onwards more detail). Outside Slam weeks: only news that affects the next Slam
  (major injuries/withdrawals of top players, retirements).
- Upcoming: next Slam with dates; during a Slam, next day's key men's/women's matches in IST.
- Exclude: ATP/WTA 250–1000 results, Davis/Laver Cup, rankings minutiae.
- Next Slams: Australian Open (Jan), Roland Garros (late May–Jun), Wimbledon (late Jun–Jul),
  US Open (late Aug–Sep).
- Good sources: atptour.com, wtatennis.com, ausopen.com, rolandgarros.com, wimbledon.com,
  usopen.org, espn.com/tennis.

## Cricket (India men first)
- Priority 1: India men's international team — every match result (score summary, top
  performers, series status) and squad/captaincy/injury news.
- Priority 2: major ICC news — upcoming World Cups, WTC final, format/schedule decisions.
- Include: India women only for tournament finals / major titles.
- Upcoming: India men's next match — opponent, format, venue, start time IST.
- Exclude: other teams' bilateral series, domestic/franchise leagues (IPL only for auction
  or major rule news), fantasy tips.
- Good sources: espncricinfo.com / cricinfo.com, bcci.tv, icc-cricket.com.
